❦ Vaagot Land

Vaagot Land is a beautiful region for all Vaags to be free. Organized in the year of our lord 2026 by a joint act between the nations of Salia Shin Gan and Mymis, Vaagot Land is now host to nine diverse nation-states. These are, in alphabetical order:

BrandonCashVille
Granch
Marquista
Muunch
Mymis
Ohgyze
Salia Shin Gan
Schmiddle Earth
St Olaf

This page exists to compile the maps and rich lore of Vaagot Land. As new discoveries are made in our archives, we'll continue to add new information. For detailed statistics or other information, we recommend studying our NationStates page.

Thank you for your interest, and enjoy your studies! We hope Vaagot Land offers you all you seek.
